# Bake-off verdict — judged by the Owner

The Owner chose instead of director-vision (brief, Phase 3 step 2). The verdict below quotes the Owner's words. The CEO turned them into requirements.

## The Owner's answer (verbatim)
> You have to be quick and you have to close the right polyps to jump on but don't be tricked they look similar but aren't and they also need to have a few possibilities and fast gameplay

## Reading
- "close the right polyps" = **choose** the right **pads**.
- **Winner: B · Lilyfield** (top-down pond, choose from up to three reachable pads, a gator that hunts you). It is the only card built on a choice at every hop, which is what the Owner asked for.

## Fold-ins (now requirements)
1. **Trick pads.** Some pads look almost like safe pads but aren't: they sink or flip when you land and drop you in the water. Each fake has a small, learnable tell (for example a slightly different tint, a curled edge, bubbles, or a faint wobble). A sharp-eyed player can spot it in under 0.5 s. A new player can be fooled once, and the next time they understand why.
2. **A few possibilities every hop.** Usually 2–3 reachable choices. At least one is always safe, so a run can never become unwinnable. The safe one is not always in the same spot.
3. **Fast.** Short hops (≤ 300 ms each) and a gator that keeps up the pressure. Hesitating is the danger, so the gator punishes standing still. Restart in under 1 s.
4. From card A: input buffering and juicy hop feel. From card C: the gator's eyes and jaws as a danger signal you read at a glance.
