## Seams found and how each was resolved
1. **Node types for `checkJs` over `scripts/` and `tests/`.** A probe showed TS 6.0.2 reports `TS2591: Cannot find name 'node:http'` without `@types/node`. I resolved it by adding `@types/node` **22.20.5** (exact pin, MIT, types only, nothing ships) and `"types": ["node"]`. This deviates from the six-pin list, so the CEO should confirm it (Requests 1).
2. **`window.__gallery` (WO-004 dev gallery) undeclared.** It caused 13 TS errors that WO-004 cannot fix from JS. I declared `__gallery: any` (dev-only) in the global `Window` in `types.d.ts`. The remaining 5 errors are WO-004's own: finding **F-001** (S2).
3. **The harness importing site modules from `/__dev/`.** Browser URLs (`/js/…`) and TS paths (`../site/js/…`) differ. The harness loads by URL with dynamic `import()` and takes its types through JSDoc `import('../site/js/types.js')`.
4. **Parallel files landing mid-WO.** My scene stubs and stub engine keep local literals (allowed by the plan) so they don't break while WO-002 changes. The harness does use WO-002's `viewport()`, since it had landed and works.
