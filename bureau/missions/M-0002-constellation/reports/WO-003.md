# Report — WO-003 — The void and its instruments (S2)

STATUS: DONE
Discipline: rendering (Three.js) + frontend UI · Mission: M-0002 · Round: build

## Built
- **Renderer** (`src/view/`): the whole void runs on one clock.
  - Nodes are SDF point sprites, with a glyph for each kind and pathway op.
  - Edges are instanced screen-space quads with width, dashes, sheen and lens-midpoint gradients.
  - Animation: ignition intro, morph with stagger and retargeting, facet ghosts and chips, route race trails with beads and chips, shortcut collapse and glint, and a pulse comet with tail, read-spark, condition chip and loop badge.
  - Camera: orbit rig with damping, focus-fly with lift, drift, and panel insets via a view offset.
- **UI** (`src/ui/`): top bar, drawer (Route · Pathways · Results), inspector, Store sheet, link mode, toasts, confirm and key-map dialogs, sound toggle (WebAudio), import/export/reset, undo, the full §12 key map, intro choreography, responsive rail and bottom sheets, and the no-WebGL state.
- **Main** (`src/main.ts`): loads the autosave or the seed through `loadSaved`/`validateDoc`, runs `startAutosave`, supports `?seed=1` and `?stress=N`, and installs `window.__constellation`.
- **Labels decision:** a capped DOM pool (40, 18 below 600 px, 48 at 1920 px). Placement is greedy by priority, and any label that overlaps a placed label or a node core is rejected (`src/view/overlay.ts`).

## Fixed after looking
- **Lines over the 64th never drew.** three.js caches `_maxInstanceCount` at first bind (fixed at `layers.ts`, the `ensure()` reset). This also hid the learned A–Z shortcut.
- **Colours were converted to linear and came out dark.** Chips rendered brick-red instead of amber.
- Ghost chips were too dim to read; the route form overflowed; the inspector actions were clipped at 1024; labels slid under the top bar on mobile; the learned line was dimmed with the rest of the sky.
- **Performance:** 3.7 → 51 fps at 500 points.
  - MSAA off (the shaders anti-alias themselves).
  - Cheaper halo.
  - Adaptive render scale down to 0.6× when fps < 40.
  - Level of detail: above 300 lines, plain 1 px "relates" lines go through native `GL_LINES`.
