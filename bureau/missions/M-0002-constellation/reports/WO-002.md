## Units design
- **Format.** `formatValue(value, unit)` is an additive export. It renders *20 °C*, *293 K* and *2 · warm*; units attach to numbers only.
- **Child words.** §5's hand-feel words are units starting with `·`, which mark scale words.
- **Flow.** A variable inherits its unit from its `read` source, or from the first operand in an `assign`. A scale word shows only beside the value it names ("3 < 5 · bubbly", U-flow-10). An `end` that builds text with `+` renders with units; that text is `Trace.result`.
- **Guard copy.** It now reads *"(heat stayed 20 °C)"*.
- **Seed.** P7 no longer appends `@unit`.
