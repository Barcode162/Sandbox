# Report — WO-003 — Hero & Combat feel

STATUS: DONE
Discipline: gameplay feel, character, input, camera, VFX, audio · Mission: M-0004 · Round: build

## Built
- **Hero "Aster".** A chibi built from merged, vertex-coloured bones on SoftToon with rim light and a screen-space ink outline. He has 13 swept spikes plus bangs, a crystal blade with a glow halo, and a 3-joint spring cape with an original chevron sigil.
- **Animation.** All of it is a pure function of action time (`hero/pose.ts`). The slash crescent (20 samples over 140 ms, hilt → 1.3× tip radius, additive gradient) is therefore exact at any instant and freezes during hit-stop.
- **Combat sim.** §5 numbers for the combo, Gale Crescent, the roll with i-frames, perfect dodge (time-scale 0.5 + afterimage), hurt and death.
- **Camera rig.** Follow, look-ahead, lock-on, orbit, title orbit and glide, kick, trauma shake and FOV punch.
- **FX.** Analytic pooled particles (≤ 400), damage numbers (stacked, comma-grouped, crits gold 40 px with a sparkle) and the hurt vignette.
- **Input.** Keyboard, mouse, gamepad, and touch (floating stick plus Attack/Dodge/Skill with cooldown sweep and onboarding cues).
- **Audio.** Full WebAudio synthesis: all SFX, procedural music layers, ambience, ducking, iOS unlock and pause fade.
