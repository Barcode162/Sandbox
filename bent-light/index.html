Colophon

Bent Light is a scroll piece in six scenes. Where you are on the page decides the picture: scroll back and it rewinds exactly; stop anywhere and it waits for you.

Seven colours, one beam, no images. Drawn with HTML, SVG and canvas, and set in your system's own sans-serif.

If you stop for a moment, the controls step aside, a label appears, and the scene breathes on top of its picture. The button in the corner stops the breathing.
