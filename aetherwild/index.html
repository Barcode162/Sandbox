  • Herbivores (Trikeros, Stegolith, Skitterbeak) tame by feeding Sunberries from bushes. Skitterbeaks are shy: crouch to sneak up.
  • Predators (Razorclaw packs, the Dreadmaw) tame by knocking them out with a Knockout Club, then feeding meat.
  • Craft a Saddle at the Workbench to ride big tames.
  • Find Aetherite in the glowing teal Aether Glade, and Void Crystal on the highest peaks.
