      Good to know

      Do crystals actually heal?

      I share what stones are traditionally associated with. They’re no substitute for medical care, and I make no health claims. Many people simply find them beautiful and grounding to have around.

      What’s in a tumbled bag?

      8–10 polished stones, about 2–3 cm each, in a cotton drawstring pouch. Perfect for a pocket, a desk, or sharing.

      What size is a big crystal, and a jar?

      A big crystal is one hand-picked piece, about 6–9 cm. A jar is a 500 ml glass jar filled with about 6 chunky pieces, 3–5 cm each.

      How does paying over time work?

      Your total splits into 4 equal payments: one today, then one every 2 weeks. No fees.

      Can I pay in person?

      Yes, for pickup or local delivery: cash or card when you collect, or when I drop it off. It isn’t available for shipped orders.

      Where do you deliver, and how long does shipping take?

      Local delivery covers about 15 miles from me and costs $5 (free over $75). Shipping takes 3–5 business days and costs $8 (free over $100). Pickup is always free.

      Will mine match the photo?

      No two stones are identical. I pick yours by hand to match its size and colour.
